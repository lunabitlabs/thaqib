About the App

Thaqib helps families reward good deeds. Kids check in their prayers, good deeds and chores, a grown-up approves, and every 10 points make a star. Stars become rewards your family chooses.

Features

  • The five daily prayers, Quran, good character, daily habits, chores and school: 44 good deeds ready to go, or your own
  • Choose what each kid sees, a whole category or deed by deed
  • Rewards kids save their stars for, and ask a grown-up for
  • Today: grown-ups approve every check-in and reward in one place
  • The whole family in sync through iCloud: up to 4 grown-ups and 6 kids, with a kid's own iPhone or iPad, or one shared by the kids
  • In English and Arabic, for iPhone and iPad

What Thaqib is

Thaqib helps families encourage good habits: kids check in when they've done something good, a grown-up approves, and stars add up to rewards the family chooses.

The Thaqib Family subscription

Thaqib Family is an auto-renewable subscription. It unlocks Thaqib for the whole family: up to 4 grown-ups and 6 kids, on any device, whichever Apple Account it uses. Shared iPads are free.

  • Price and length: the yearly price is shown in the app before you buy, in your own currency. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free month ends.
  • Free month: new subscribers may start with one month free. Unless it's cancelled at least 24 hours before the month ends, the yearly subscription begins and is charged.
  • Renewal: the subscription renews each year unless it's cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current year. The renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that.
  • Cancelling: in the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions; or in Thaqib, Settings › Subscription › Manage in the App Store. The subscription then runs until the end of the period already paid for.
  • Family Sharing: Thaqib Family can be shared with your Apple family.
  • When it ends: Thaqib rests. Nothing is deleted; the kids still see their stars and their sky, and check-ins, approvals and rewards wait until someone in the family subscribes again.
  • Founding families: if you bought Thaqib before it became a subscription, your family keeps everything, free, for good.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, under its own terms.

Families and supervision

A parent or guardian sets up the family and is responsible for how Thaqib is used with their children, including who is invited, what is rewarded, and keeping the family PIN private. Rewards come from your family; we don't provide or fulfil them.

Religious content

The deeds Thaqib suggests for Prayers, Quran and Character & manners support your family's practice; they aren't religious rulings. Thaqib doesn't calculate prayer times. For religious guidance, consult a qualified scholar.

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Thaqib helps families reward good habits. This policy explains what the app keeps, where it's kept, and who can see it.

In short: your family's information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, shared only with the people you invite. Lunabit Labs, which makes Thaqib, doesn't collect it and can't see it.

What Thaqib keeps

  • Your kids' names, colors and emoji, and, if you add them, a photo, their age, and whether each is a boy or a girl (used only for the Arabic wording).
  • The behaviors and rewards you set up, what each kid sees, and the reward each kid is saving for.
  • Check-ins, approvals, stars and redeemed rewards, with when each happened, which family device made each check-in, and which grown-up decided, by the name the kids call them (such as Mom).
  • The family PIN, kept only as a salted one-way hash, never the PIN itself.
  • On each device, who uses it (a grown-up, one kid, or the kids) and, on a grown-up's device, the name the kids call them, kept in that device's Keychain.
  • Whether the family's subscription is on and until when, and the name of the grown-up who pays (as the kids call them), so every device in the family knows.

Where it's kept

On your devices, and in the iCloud account of the grown-up who set up the family, through Apple's CloudKit. Apple protects iCloud data in transit and at rest. Thaqib has no servers of its own, and nothing is sent to us.

Who can see it

Only the people you invite. Each invite is made for one person and works once. Grown-ups you invite can see and change the family's information; a kid's own device shows that kid's page. In parent mode, Settings → Manage People shows who has joined, and lets you remove anyone.

What we collect

Nothing. Thaqib has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. We don't sell or share your information, because we never receive it.

If you write to us from Thaqib, the email includes the app's version, your device's model and its iOS version, and a few lines about how your family's sync and notifications are working: counts and times, never names. You see all of it before you send.

Children

Thaqib is meant to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian. Kids' information is entered by a grown-up and stays in the family's iCloud; it is never sent to us. Kids don't create accounts in Thaqib.

Purchases

The subscription is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment: Thaqib never sees your payment details. Thaqib only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account has the subscription, or bought Thaqib before it became one. Kids never see a price.

Permissions

  • Camera: only on the screen that scans an invite code (Join with a code), to read the code. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Notifications: to tell grown-ups about check-ins and requests waiting for them, kids about their approved stars, and the grown-up who started the free month that it ends in two days. They're created on your devices, not sent from a server.
  • Photos: Thaqib uses the system photo picker, so it only ever sees the photo you choose for a kid.
  • Face ID and Touch ID: handled by iOS; Thaqib never receives biometric data.

Deleting your data

In parent mode, open Settings → Kids' Data. On a device signed in to the Apple Account that set up the family, Erase All Data deletes the family's information from iCloud and from every device in the family. On a device that joined a family, Leave the Family takes that Apple Account out of the family, and the family's information off every device signed in to it; the family stays for everyone else. The grown-up who set up the family can also remove anyone in Manage People.

You can also delete Thaqib's data from your iCloud in the Settings app: your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. On the Apple Account that set up the family, this deletes the family for everyone in it.
